# AgriMart 🌾🚜

**AgriMart** is a comprehensive, farmer-focused agricultural marketplace and equipment rental platform designed to empower agricultural communities, increase equipment access, and streamline crop and product sales.

---

## 📌 Features Overview

- **Authentication & User Profiles**: Multi-role platform for Farmers, Equipment Owners, Produce Buyers, and Admins.
- **Agricultural Equipment Rental**: Rent tractors, harvesters, seeders, and sprayers per day or hour.
- **Used Equipment Marketplace**: Buy and sell pre-owned agricultural machinery with condition reports and pricing.
- **Agricultural Products Marketplace**: Browse and purchase fertilizers, seeds, pesticides, and farming tools.
- **Crop Marketplace**: Direct farm-to-buyer crop listing with bulk quantity pricing and harvest dates.
- **Location-Based Listings**: GPS-assisted search, district/city filters, and distance radius matching.
- **Rental & Purchase Management**: Real-time booking requests, order tracking, and purchase inquiries.
- **Communication & Reviews**: Direct in-app messaging and transparent rating systems.
- **User & Admin Dashboards**: Managed listings, booking history, analytics, and platform moderation.
